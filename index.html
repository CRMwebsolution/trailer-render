How these estimates work

Weights use approximate material and component allowances. Axles sit at 60% of deck length. The empty hitch estimate assumes 12.5% for bumper pull or 22% for gooseneck. Load placement, vehicle capacity, turning clearance and structural strength are not calculated. Use manufacturer ratings and actual measurements for real equipment. Exported models are visualization meshes, not fabrication plans.
